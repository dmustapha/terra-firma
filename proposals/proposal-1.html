Terra Firma, Proposal 1: Survey Sheet

Every screen is a drawing sheet from the survey office at the edge of the flat world. Paper with real contour lines, a ruled frame with zone references, and a title block that names the datum: Robinhood Chain and Chainlink.

The Hindsight card is drawn as a cross-section. The meme layer has eroded to a sliver, NVDA holds as bedrock, and what was paid runs across as the datum line. Every boundary carries a leader line to where it was read from.

Color

paper ink #C2410C 55% 32% 12% 6%

Type

IBM Plex Serif for display and text.
IBM Plex Mono for every measured number, tabular.

Grid: Survey drawing sheet

Double-ruled frame, numbered and lettered zone strip, title block bottom right. Dense, information-forward. The 375 frame drops the zone strip and keeps the frame and title block.

Motion and contrast

State-driven only: layers settle once on load, the active borehole drills while a token is being read. Off under reduced motion. Text pairs pass WCAG AA (ink 15.9:1, muted 6.4:1, vermilion 4.5:1).
