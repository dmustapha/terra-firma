# WIF Hackathon — Rules extract (source: https://worldisflat.fun/build/rules, read 2026-11-10 06:15 UTC, public — no sign-in needed) [A1]

## What to build
"build a product on Robinhood Chain that people keep coming back to ... as long as it puts $wif to work inside it, and it needs a flywheel"

## Rules (numbered as on page)
1. Solo or team ≤4; one team per person.
2. Free entry; never need to hold anything. Winner picked "on the work itself, by the judges, with the community vote as one seat among them."
3. **Project has to use $wif onchain on Robinhood Chain.**
4. **Must stay live and working until the final closes (16 Nov 17:30 UTC)** — they will try it.
5. **Proof: visible repo + demo video + real transactions.**
6. Existing work welcome if it puts $wif to work in a real way.
7. **Needs a flywheel** — usage keeps bringing people back to $wif.
8. WIF team/judges/households can't enter.
9. Edits lock at submission close; only the logo changes until final close.
10. 18+.
11. No Robinhood name/logo in names/logos/handles; follow RH brand guidelines (docs.robinhood.com/chain/brand-guidelines).
12. No harassment/spam/impersonation in comments.
13. No malware / no IP misuse.
14. Stock Tokens category → follow Robinhood restricted-country list (docs.robinhood.com/rhj/restricted-jurisdictions).
15. Winner confirms by email: 18+, not sanctioned, not in sanctioned country; sends each member's name+country+payout wallet.
16. Can't pay → prize to next finalist.
17. Organizer may pause/change/cancel on fraud/technical failure.

## Prize
10,000 USDG, single winner, no second place, paid to team lead wallet.

## How judging works
1. Organizers review every submission; approved ones go to the vote.
2. Judges vote; **community votes with $wif and counts as ONE more judge**. Top 10 by votes → finalists.
3. Judges + community vote again on finalists; most votes wins.
- Every $wif used to vote is burned. Votes for later-rejected projects don't count; burned $wif not returned.
- "judges look for a real reason to use $wif, a flywheel that holds up, and something that works and is built well."

## FAQ

- Team? ≤4, prize to lead's wallet.
- Community voting: connect wallet, vote with $wif, 1 $wif = 1 vote, burned; community top picks count as one judge.
- Change submission? Until close, then locks.
- Private repo? Yes — invite GitHub `wifonrh`.
- Visibility: after approval, name/one-liner/categories/team/updates public; what-it-does, demo, live link public at submission close; rest judges-only.
- Not affiliated with Robinhood Markets.
- $wif = the voting token, no promise of value.
