# DECISIONS: Terra Firma demo film

1. **The voice is master; pictures follow it.** Every kept take (voice/selection.json) is cleaned with the voicebooth chain (trim, high-pass 80 Hz, denoise, de-ess, session pitch/pace/tone match, clarity EQ, compression, -16 LUFS) by `scripts/voice_assemble.py` and placed on its cue (cues.json; a line that runs long pushes the next one 0.25 s later). The voice is never moved or stretched. Word times come from local Whisper (medium) on the assembled voice; script words are matched to heard words, so a mis-heard word ("wef", "pawns") still keeps its slot.
2. **Surgical mapping by anchors.** `scripts/plan.py` pins each named word to the source second where the screen shows that thing (build/anchors.json), then solves the picture speed between anchors. Speeds stay within 0.8x to 3x; anything slower becomes 0.8x and then a held frame of the landing moment; anything faster gets an explicit jump cut (dissolved over 5 frames). Sped spans of 1.5x or more carry a small muted corner tag (for example "2x"), never a banner.
3. **No re-cut from raw takes.** Picture comes only from the locked `rough_cut_v4.mp4` (frame-checked clean of MetaMask, VS Code and desktop) and the four clean dark captures in `recordings/extra/`. The extras had non-zero start timestamps and full-range color; they are re-encoded once (start at 0, limited range) so Remotion and ffmpeg agree on every frame time.
4. **Wallet-confirm cards replace every hold.** Each frozen placeholder in v4 becomes a paper card ("Confirmed in wallet ✓", the action, and the real short tx hash from the mainnet logs) over the frozen last clean frame. Approvals carry no hash (the approve is on the token contract). The sell's two signatures are shown as one card ("approve + sell PONS") so the cut lands "this is where wif comes in" on the $WIF row. TIMING-PLAN.md listed the convert hash as 0x9425…b037; the full hash in the logs ends 7108, so the film uses **0x9425…7108**.
5. **Frozen frames are PNG stills**, extracted with ffmpeg at the exact source second, because a held video frame inside Remotion drifted.
6. **Overlays on footage, never slides.** Hook count-up (the card's own numbers: $18,421 paid, $0.35 now, $16,972 in NVDA, counted on the words that name them), system labels drawn onto the home page's sediment / sieve / bedrock bands, the $WIF flywheel drawn over the distribute footage it describes, who-it's-for chips over the leaderboard tubes, an honest "what it doesn't claim" chip, and the close plate over the home page's own "Stand on bedrock." band.
7. **Brand.** Almanac paper (#F1EBDD) tags with ink borders and pixel-offset shadows, moss for confirmed, rust for the eroded meme layer, Instrument Serif + Public Sans, the seal from web/public/logo.svg. Transitions: no strata wipes (the full-frame tricolour bands read as a flag); 12-frame crossfades at every chapter change, 5-frame dissolves on jump cuts; focus zooms move the named element toward the centre without ever showing a frame edge.
8. **Close.** A 6 s end card on a darkened, blurred background with one ask in Dami's voice: "paste your wallet. stake $WIF. get paid in stock.", plus terrafirma-wif.vercel.app and github.com/dmustapha/terra-firma. The seconds come from trimming the empty leaderboard and dispatches holds.
9. **Robinhood rules.** "Robinhood Chain" in full, "Stock Tokens", no $HOOD, no Robinhood logo, no implied endorsement.
10. **Music.** Ducking is a drawn gain envelope from the placed voice lines: -7 dB under every line (150 ms attack, 400 ms release), +2 dB lift on the flywheel, +3 dB on the end card, 120 Hz high-pass. Bed choice: Working bed: Digital Technology by AtlasAudio (Pixabay Content License), reused from the Plumbline film (~/vultr-rush/video/DECISIONS.md #5 confirms the track and license). It is 284 s long, so no loop is needed; it fades in over 1.5 s, is side-chain ducked under the voice and fades out with the picture. Four licensed alternatives are in MUSIC-OPTIONS.md.
11. **Loudness.** Final mix normalised with one linear gain to -16 LUFS integrated (web), true peak limited below -1 dBTP.
12. **Privacy.** The voice takes stay in voice/takes/ (gitignored) and were never uploaded. Renders and clips stay out of git (video/build/, video/out/, remotion/public/clips/).
13. **Review round (REVIEW-A / REVIEW-B).** Picture is cropped 4% from the top centre so Chrome's bottom-left link-hover bubble never shows. The app floors "left on the table" to $0 on small cards; the WALLET card gets an exact-value patch ($1.22 in NVDA minus $0.60 today = $0.62) and the PONS card's top half is trimmed so its $0 never shows. "this is my own wallet on mainnet" now sits on the connected 0x7204..05aa trade page. Half-loaded frames (Jar skeleton, Stake stats, empty leaderboard cores) are cut or replaced by loaded frames. Captions stay in one place at the bottom and capitalise proper nouns (Robinhood Chain, Terra Firma, NVDA, $WIF, Chainlink, Uniswap, Stock Tokens, PONS). A $WIF chip lands by 0:26 ("every trade buys $WIF and burns it"). Frame 0 is the settled $16,971 card.
14. **Stake tx.** Checked on Robinhood Chain: nonce 22 is the approve 0xa63c…5077 and nonce 23 is the mined stake 0x5a0f…6f11 (to the staking contract). The app's status line shows 0xaae4…6fe8, the hash the wallet first broadcast for nonce 23 before it was replaced, so it is not onchain. The card shows the approve and the mined stake. Every other card hash was re-checked by RPC: same sender 0x7204..05aa, status 1.
