# DESIGN_BRIEF: Terra Firma

## IDENTITY
- world-statement: Ground, not glitter. Robinhood Chain traders live on a flat world of memes; Terra Firma is the survey office at the edge of it, where every number is measured from the chain like an elevation and the user's dead memes settle into bedrock as real Stock Tokens.
- accent: one earth accent per direction (survey vermilion #C2410C, ochre #C8901B, or bedrock moss #3F6B4A, decided at selection); never Robin Neon #ccff00 (that is Robinhood Chain's own brand color and would imply endorsement), never neon-on-black.
- signature element: the Strata readout, a Hindsight card drawn as a measured cross-section where the meme layer erodes and the stock layer holds, with real USD at each boundary.
- rejection list: no stats ticker or marquee, no uppercase mono eyebrow, no icon+title+subtitle feature boxes, no 3-step "how it works" grid, no glass cards, no purple/blue gradients, no neon, no giant hero numerals as decoration, no "tokenized stocks" wording, no Robinhood marks, no invented metrics.
- quality-bar: best product in a 31-entry field; every viewport should read like a printed survey document a judge wants to screenshot.
- aesthetic-fit: forensic finance meets field geology; precise, warm, calm, trustworthy, never casino.

## HERO
money-shot: the Strata readout of a real wallet ($15,380.80 into memes, $18.52 today, $15,016.29 had it been NVDA; re-read live).
entry-funnel: Connect wallet (primary) → your card; secondary: look up any wallet.
honesty-placement: directly under the headline, the guarantee line ("$WIF is never sold; every number is read from Robinhood Chain and Chainlink") with a link to /proof.
invariant-as-narrative: "measured, not estimated": each number on the cross-section carries its source (tx, Chainlink round).
